controlled clocks, coroutine signals and injected sleepers. JVM and Android are
the supported targets. No live server, socket or wall-clock threshold is evidence
required by this contract.

## Transport scope

HTTP stack compression/framing and native socket-timeout enforcement are properties
of the supplied engine boundary. Tests supply decoded entity bytes, explicit
truncated EOF and typed timeouts, while the public library controls classification,
admission, immutable payloads, cancellation, disposal and lifecycle. No transport
stub is treated as upstream-provider verification. The freshly packaged consumer
uses the same versioned contract replies without compiling library source or
substituting projects. Its result is reported separately from library verification.
